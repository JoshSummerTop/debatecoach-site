Lessons

Watch a short lesson. Then do it.

Each lesson is a few minutes of video from a debate coach, a short read and a quick check. Most end with a practice that's already set up, so you try the idea while it's fresh.

  • Courses that start with what a motion asks and build to weighing
  • A drill or a speech ready at the end of most lessons
What to practise next

Every report sets up the next practice.

The coach reads your last report and suggests what to work on today. If your rebuttals named the point but not why it fails, today's practice is a rebuttal drill.

  • Today's practice, picked from your last report
  • Every report kept in one place to read again

Questions

Before you start

Who is Debate Coach for?

It's for students learning to speak and argue in English, from a first conversation to a full World Schools round.

Do I need any experience?

No. The coach explains each round before it starts and gives an example the first time you try something new.

What do I need?

You need a phone and a quiet place to talk. Earphones help, but you don't need them.

Do I talk to real people?

No. Your opponent and your coach are AI, so you can practise whenever you like.

How much does it cost?

Prices will be in the App Store and Google Play when the app launches.

Which languages does it speak?

The practice is always in English. The app and the coach's notes also come in Chinese, Korean, Japanese, Vietnamese, Spanish and Portuguese.

When is it available?

Debate Coach is in testing now. It's coming to iPhone and Android.
