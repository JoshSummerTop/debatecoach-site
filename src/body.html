Questions

Who is Debate Coach for?

It's for anyone who wants to get better at debating or speak more clearly, from a first presentation to a World Schools round, a job interview or a meeting in English. You need to be 13 or older.

Do I need debate experience?

No. Your coach starts with one question and one minute, and builds from there. Full debates are there when you want them.

What do I need?

You need a phone and a quiet place to talk. Earphones help, but you don't need them.

Do I talk to other people?

No. Your coach and your opponents are AI, so you can practise whenever you like.

How much does it cost?

Prices will be in the App Store and Google Play when the app launches.

Which languages does it support?

Every practice and lesson is in English. You speak English, and so do your coach and your opponents. The menus and buttons, your written report and the weekly note can also be shown in Simplified or Traditional Chinese, Korean, Japanese, Vietnamese, Spanish or Portuguese.

Which debate formats are there?

World Schools, British Parliamentary, Public Forum and Lincoln-Douglas, among others, with AI speakers in every other seat.

When can I get it?

Debate Coach is in testing now. It's coming to iPhone and Android.
