Questions

I've never debated. Is it for me?

Yes. Level 1 is one minute on an everyday question, and your coach explains each new skill when it arrives.

Is AI feedback any good?

Every report marks the five things a debate coach listens for and gives you one change to try straight away. Your first five practices are free, so you can judge for yourself.

Who hears my recordings?

No person. Only the app's AI hears them: your coach, and the opponent in a round. They stay in your account until you delete them.

Which languages does it support?

You practise in English. Menus and reports can also be shown in Simplified or Traditional Chinese, Korean, Japanese, Vietnamese, Spanish or Portuguese.

Can I debate a full round?

Not in the app: it practises one skill at a time, up to mini rounds. Full rounds in six formats, judged speech by speech, are Shadow Judge, which runs debates online. The link is at the top of the page.

How old do I need to be?

13 or older.
