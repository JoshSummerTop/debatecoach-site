Privacy

Last changed 28 October 2026. This page says what the app keeps, why, and how to delete it.

What the app keeps

  • Your account: an email address (or your Apple or Google sign-in), a password if you choose one, your date of birth, which we ask to check you are 13 or older, the name your coach calls you if you give one, and what you told the app you want to get better at. You can use the app for a first practice before making an account.
  • Your practices: each practice, round and live talk is recorded so your coach can hear it. The app keeps the recording, the transcript of what was said, the report your coach wrote, and your messages to your coach in the chat. They are in your account and no person sees them, unless you send us a problem report about one, which we open only to fix the problem.
  • Your settings: the language you chose for menus and reports, and the time of your daily reminder, with a device token so the reminder reaches your phone.
  • How the app is used: which screens are opened and when practices start and finish, so we can see what helps people practise. It never includes your recordings or what you said.
  • The usual technical facts: the time a request was made, the kind of phone and a number for this install of the app, so we can fix faults and offer one practice before an account on each phone.

Where it goes

Your voice in a practice is carried by LiveKit, written down by a speech-to-text service (AssemblyAI), and answered by the AI opponent and coach (OpenAI); your report and the coach's chat answers are written by an AI (Anthropic). Each of these receives only what it needs for that practice, and none of them keeps your recordings to train their models. Your account, recordings and reports are stored with Supabase. Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared with advertisers.

How long it stays

Your recordings and reports stay in your account until you delete them or delete your account. A recording is kept so you can replay it and so your coach's report has something to point to.

Payments

Plans and packs are bought through Apple or Google, which handle your payment. RevenueCat, which manages subscriptions for us, receives a record of the purchase and your app account number. We never see your card details.

Deleting

You can delete your account from inside the app, under You. That removes your account, your recordings, your transcripts and your reports. If you cannot open the app, write to the address below and we will do it for you.

Children

Debate Coach is for people aged 13 and over. We ask for a date of birth when an account is made and refuse one for anyone younger.
