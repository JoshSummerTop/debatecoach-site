Privacy

Last changed 26 October 2026. This page says what the app keeps, why, and how to delete it.

What the app keeps

  • Your account: an email address and a password, and your date of birth, which we ask to check you are 13 or older. You can use the app for a first practice before making an account.
  • Your practices: each round is recorded so your coach can hear it. The app keeps the recording, the transcript of what was said, and the card or report your coach wrote. They are in your account and nobody else sees them.
  • Your settings: the language you chose for menus and notes.
  • The usual technical facts: the time a request was made and the kind of phone, so we can fix faults.

Where it goes

Debate Coach runs on Shadow Judge, the AI debate-judging platform. Your voice in a round is carried by LiveKit, written down by a speech-to-text service (Deepgram or AssemblyAI), answered by an AI opponent (OpenAI) and judged by an AI coach (Anthropic). Each of these receives only what it needs for that round, and none of them keeps your recordings to train their models. Your account, recordings and cards are stored with Supabase. Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared with advertisers.

How long it stays

Your recordings and cards stay in your account until you delete them or delete your account. A recording is kept so you can replay it and so your coach's card has something to point to.

Deleting

You can delete your account from inside the app, under You. That removes your account, your recordings, your transcripts and your cards. If you cannot open the app, write to the address below and we will do it for you.

Children

Debate Coach is for people aged 13 and over. We ask for a date of birth when an account is made and refuse one for anyone younger.
